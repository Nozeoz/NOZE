# 02 — Story & World

> **TL;DR:** A thousand years ago you were the Last Sovereign of **Halcyon**, the greatest kingdom that ever stood. On its final night you spoke the **Last Decree**, and the Crown shattered. You wake today in the **Veilwilds**, a mist-drowned wilderness you don't recognise, in a new body, with only a talking ember called **Flicker** for company. You survive, you build, and slowly you learn three truths: this wilderness *is* Halcyon; the mist was *your* doing; and the power you're reclaiming is the only thing holding back what lies beneath.
>
> ⚠️ **Spoilers.** This doc is for the dev team and contains the full mystery.

---

## 1. Premise (player-facing)

> *You were a legend. Songs were written about your reign. Then came the last night, the fire, and a light so bright it erased everything.*
>
> *Now you wake in wet grass beside a broken stone throne. Your body is young and weak. Mist rolls over a land you've never seen. A tiny flame with a tiny crown hovers over your face and says, "Oh good, you're not dead. Again."*
>
> *You have no army, no crown, no name anyone remembers. But you remember how to rule, and this land needs a ruler.*

---

## 2. Themes & tone

| Theme | Expressed through |
|---|---|
| **Second chances** | The Sovereign's rebirth; spared enemies who become family |
| **Power vs. people** | The old Sovereign ruled alone and paid for it; the new one must rely on others |
| **The cost of choices** | The Last Decree saved the world and drowned a kingdom |
| **Memory & time** | A thousand years passed; long-lived friends remember what you forgot |
| **Community** | Food, festivals, and small kindnesses are what a kingdom is actually made of |

**Tone:** about 70% warm slice-of-life (banter, food, blossoms, romance) and 30% mystery, melancholy, and danger. Humour mostly comes from Flicker and the cast. Anime sensibility: dramatic reveals, heartfelt confessions, rivals who become friends, transformation moments.

**Hard rules:**
- No gore; defeated creatures dissolve into mist.
- No named character dies on-screen during normal play (one historical tragedy is shown only in flashback).
- Villains have understandable motives.

## 3. The world at a glance

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Veilwilds** | The present-day name of the land: a region covered by the **Veil**, a living mist. The whole game takes place here. |
| **Halcyon** | The kingdom that stood here 1,000 years ago. Remembered only as a legend: *"the Drowned Kingdom."* In-world, "halcyon days" still means "a golden age." |
| **The Outer Realms** | Lands beyond the Veil. Home of the **Aldmark Dominion** and other nations. Most outsiders who enter the Veil never return. |
| **The Below / the Maw** | An ancient, mindless Hunger beneath the world that consumes life, memory, and meaning. |

### Cosmology (how magic works)

- **The Heartflame** is the living fire the first sovereigns kindled to keep the Hunger asleep. It lived inside the **Crown of Halcyon**.
- **The Veil** is a mist woven from the shattered Crown. It **seals** the Maw beneath the old capital, but it also swallowed the kingdom. It thickens at night (the **Gloaming**) and near the Maw.
- **Veilkin** are creatures born over centuries where Veil magic mixed with nature. They are not evil; the Veil is their home. They can form **Pacts** with the Sovereign, whose reborn body is partly made of Veil (see §6).
- **The Hollowed** are Veilkin (and sometimes people) consumed by the Hunger leaking through the weakening seal. White hollow eyes, violet-teal mist, aggressive. **They can be purified** with Heartflame light.
- **Elements:** Terra, Aqua, Ignis, Zephyr, Lumen, Umbra, plus the **Veil** as a corrupting non-element. Opposite pairs: Ignis⇄Aqua, Terra⇄Zephyr, Lumen⇄Umbra. Lumen purifies the Veil.

## 5. The mystery, in four layers

| Layer | Question | Revealed in | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Where am I?"** | Ch1–2 | Unknown land; Flicker has lost its memory too. Memory Fragments show glimpses of a grand golden city. |
| 2 | **"This is Halcyon."** | Ch3 (midpoint) | In the Drowned Bells you find your own royal seal on a sunken cathedral. Elowen admits she knew. The Veil is a thousand years old and you were the Last Sovereign. |
| 3 | **"I made the Veil."** | Ch5 | Vesper reveals the Last Decree: *you* drowned your people to seal the Maw. And the Regalia you've been reclaiming **are the Veil's anchors**. Every one you took weakened the seal, which is why the Surges have been growing. Flicker hid this memory **because your past self asked it to.** |
| 4 | **"What kind of Sovereign will I be?"** | Ch6 | The final choice (see §10). |

**Design payoff:** layer 3 re-frames the difficulty curve. The Hollowed got stronger *because* you got stronger. This is dramatic irony the player can feel in hindsight.

## 6. The protagonist: the Sovereign

- **Player-named.** Title chosen at creation: *King / Queen / Monarch*. Pronouns: he / she / they.
- **Reborn body.** The Heartflame rebuilt a young adult body (~20 years old) out of Veil essence. This is why the Sovereign can form **Pacts** with Veilkin: they sense kinship.
- **Royal Rights** (lore basis for the mechanics):
  - **The Right of Naming:** a sovereign's name carries power. Naming a bonded familiar causes **Ascension**.
  - **The Right of Knighthood:** raising a loyal companion to Knight unlocks their full potential.
  - **The Right of Decree:** words spoken from the throne reshape how the realm lives.
- **Diegetic character creation:** after waking, you look into a pond. *"Who are you now?"* The reflection is the character creator. Flicker asks for a name: *"Your old one's a bit… legendary."*

### Flicker, the guide
The last spark of the Heartflame: a palm-sized floating flame with a tiny crown. Snarky, gluttonous for Lumen crystals, loyal to the bone. It teaches systems, comments on everything, and grows with every Regalia recovered. Its secret: it carries the memories your past self chose to forget. Full profile in [`04_CHARACTERS.md`](04_CHARACTERS.md).

### Why the villains do what they do
- **Vesper** lost her beloved to the Hollowing a thousand years ago. She believes all suffering comes from clinging, and that the Maw's silence is mercy. She sees the Veil, your Decree, as the cruelty that trapped everyone in a millennium of grief.
- **Lord-Marshal Thane** is not a monster; he's a pragmatist. Veilglass powers his nation's winters. He sees your realm as squatters on a resource.
- **Caelan (the Ashen Warden)** kept his oath for a thousand years alone at the seal until the Hunger hollowed him out. He attacks you because what's left of him can no longer tell you from the Maw.

### Travel & gating: Beacon Roads
The deep Veil between regions is impassable at first: a **Veil Wall** of choking mist. To open a region you need **(a)** the previous Regalia, and **(b)** a **Waybeacon** built at the border, which clears the road for good and doubles as a fast-travel point. *Survival → building → exploration* in one mechanic.

### Prologue beat sheet: *The Last Decree* (≈5 min)
1. Black screen, a lullaby on music box (the future **Flicker motif**).
2. The Sovereign, at full power, fights through the burning capital beside **Sir Caelan**. The player has every Royal Art unlocked: a power fantasy that teaches combat.
3. Tendrils of the Maw erupt; Hollowed pour from the streets. Caelan: *"Go. I'll hold the gate. I'll always hold the gate."*
4. The throne room. **Vesper** blocks the way, pleading. The Sovereign walks past her.
5. The Sovereign raises the Crown: *"By the Crown of Halcyon, I decree—"* Everything goes white. **The decree itself is never heard.** (It's revealed in Ch5.)
6. Title card.

## 11. Endings (1.0)

| Ending | Condition | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **A: The Eternal Flame** | Always available | You reforge the Crown and become the seal's eternal keeper. The Veil remains, stable. Your Consort (if any) rules as regent. Bittersweet. |
| **B: Halcyon Dawn** (true ending) | ≥16 Sworn recruited, ≥6 Knights, average Loyalty ≥70 | You unmake the Veil and the **whole realm** fights the Maw: every Sworn, familiar, and settler you earned. The land is restored. Caelan lives if purified. |
| **C: The Hearth Commonwealth** (secret) | Ending B conditions, plus the *Mercy Edict* and *Scholars' Charter* each enacted at least once, top Prosperity, and high Court approval | After victory you place the Crown on a round table and found a council. Epilogue: you tend your fields, *"the Sovereign Who Chose the Soil."* The farm-game thesis made literal. |

If you attempt B without enough strength, the battle turns and you are forced into A. **No Sworn dies**; the cost is the ending, not their lives.

**After the credits:** play continues the next morning (Stardew-style). Endings B and C lift the Veil from restored regions, so nights are safe everywhere. Ending A keeps the night Veil. In all endings **Veil Rifts** (endless post-game dungeons) remain as the last echoes of the Maw.

## 12. Memory Fragments (collectible lore)

30 fragments in total: 4 per dungeon for dungeons 1–5 (20), 6 in Halcyon Below, and 4 hidden in the overworld. Each fragment is a short in-engine vignette in the sepia "memory" shader that reuses existing assets. They reveal, in order: the golden age → Caelan's oath → Elowen and Vesper as young court members → the Hollowing → the Last Night from other perspectives → the Decree itself (final fragment, in Ch5).

Donating fragments to the **Royal Archive** (Elowen) gives **Lore**, which is used for research.

## 13. How the story is delivered

| Channel | Used for | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| In-engine cutscenes (sprites, camera moves, emotes, portraits) | Main story beats | Medium |
| Heart events | Character arcs, romance | Medium |
| Court Day petitions | Small stories of ordinary settlers | Low (templated) |
| Memory Fragments | Backstory | Low |
| Flicker commentary | Tutorials, humor, hints | Low |
| Item and compendium descriptions | World texture | Very low |
| Event CGs (pixel illustrations) | 10-heart moments, weddings, endings | High (1.0 stretch) |

### English writing style guide
- **Modern, warm, readable English** with light fantasy flavour. No "thee/thou" except **Caelan** (formal, old-court speech) and prologue royal ceremony.
- Short lines. A dialogue box holds at most ~3 lines. The player reads on a phone-sized screen too.
- **Every character has voice markers** (defined in the cast bible): e.g. Rook uses nicknames and sarcasm; Elowen uses precise vocabulary and sighs; Kaida speaks in challenges; Flicker uses exclamations and food metaphors.
- Humor is character-based, never mean to the player.
- No real-world slang that dates quickly. Light swearing only ("damn", "hells").

## 14. Level design overview: Dawnmere Vale

```
                         N ↑
      ~~~~~~~~ VEIL WALL (to Whisperwood) ~~~~~~~~
                   [Old Bridge]
                        │
   [Windmill Ruin]──────┼──────────────[Ruined Chapel]
         │         ~~~~ River ~~~~             │
   [Old Royal Fields]   │            [Mossbun Meadow]
         │         ┌────┴────┐               │
         │         │ ASHEN   │  ← capital site, Beacon center
         │         │ THRONE  │               │
         │         └────┬────┘        [Cliff Road] → (Saltglass Coast, E)
   [Sunken Cellars]  [Reflection Pond]
     (dungeon)          │
                [Caravan Crossroads] → (mountain pass to Cinderpeak, S)
```

- **Realm growth:** the Beacon's radius grows **10 → 18 → 28 → 40 tiles** across Beacon tiers 1–4; Watchtowers add +8-tile bubbles. The realm is the union of these circles, so the player shapes their borders.
- **Space budget at Kingdom rank:** ~20 homes, ~30 service/production buildings, royal fields, decorations. It fits inside the tier-4 Beacon radius plus two towers.
- **Night readability:** light sources carve safe shapes out of the Veil. The player *sees* their territory at night.
